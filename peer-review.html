Editorial & Peer Review Process

  1. Submission – author submits manuscript online and pays screening fee.
  2. Screening – originality, serious flaws and plagiarism checked.
  3. Editor assessment – accept for review or reject (about 7 days).
  4. Peer review – 2 to 5 external reviewers, double-blind (6–8 weeks).
  5. Decision – publish unaltered, minor changes, major changes, or reject.
  6. Revision – author responds to comments (3 weeks).
  7. Final acceptance – editor re-checks (2 weeks).
  8. Production – typesetting, author proof, corrections (about 4 weeks).
  9. Publication – issue release or Online First.
